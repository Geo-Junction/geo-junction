Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F d, Y" %}

At Aplus Initiative, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your CBU student number (if provided)
  • Your school and year of study
  • Information you provide when booking tutoring sessions
  • Your interactions with our platform (courses viewed, tests taken, etc.)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our tutoring services
  • To personalize your learning experience
  • To communicate with you about your bookings and account
  • To send you updates, announcements, and promotional materials (with your consent)
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve our platform

3. How We Protect Your Data

  • We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data
  • We store personal information securely and limit access to authorized personnel
  • We never sell your personal information to third parties
  • We comply with all applicable data protection laws

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (e.g., payment processors, email services) to support our platform. These services have their own privacy policies and data protection practices.

5. Your Rights

  • You can access, update, or delete your personal information at any time
  • You can opt out of marketing communications
  • You can request a copy of the data we hold about you

6. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience, remember your preferences, and analyze site usage. You can control cookie settings through your browser.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
